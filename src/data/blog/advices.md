**If you're 10-20:**

- **Find something that interest you.** I recommend to start with computers. because computers is a big tree and you can choose a branch and becoming an expert at it.
- **Talk to strangers.** Moms doesn't like this but it helps you to open your mind to different perspective and if you can see the world with different glasses, you will make less mistakes.
- **Read books.** Books teaches me to focus. Avoid social media. Consume only what you want. Don't be into the algorithm trap. There are some of the best engineers who their work is to make your facovirte app addictive and grow the revenu of their company. Don't fall into that. Be careful.
- **Move.** Make a sport. Strech a muscle. Take a ball. Run. Swim. Grimpe. Move. Your brain needs it. If you want to get succefful or whatever. You need to think clearly.- **Don't play too much video games.** I was addict to Call Of Duty to earning new camouflage and levels. Play at life. You can win more and it can be more addictive. Try to earn a degree. A partner. A call. A talk. A conversation. A business. Money.
- **Learn to focus long hours.** Maybe you want to be rich. Maybe you want to have succefsul school notes. In both case, you need to have a long time attention. So eat clean and move. 
- Eat clean. You are what you eat. Yes I eat burgers and pizza from time to time but try to have a 80% clean diet. Clean mean natural to me. If you know the plant or the animal without looking at the package it's clean. 
- Use the internet correctly. I meet so much interesting people in the internet. Just send message to who inspires you something like "hey I saw you contributed at XYZ, I'm very curious about your learning process, Do you have any project or book that helps you i?
