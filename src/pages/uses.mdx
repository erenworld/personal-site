# Currently enjoying

- [M3 MacBook Pro](https://www.apple.com/macbook-pro/) - hands down my best investment
- [LG 34" UltraWide display](https://www.lg.com/us/monitors/ultrawide-monitors) - after two years I find it a bit too big for my setup, but it still does the job
- [Mechanical keyboard by Logitech](https://www.logitech.com/fr-fr/shop/p/mx-mechanical-mini.920-010776) - satisfying to type on, holds up well daily
- [Ikea MITTZON standing desk](https://www.ikea.com/fr/fr/p/mittzon-bureau-assis-debout-electrique-plaque-noyer-blanc-s29530344/) - solid, no-frills desk that just works
- [Ghostty](https://ghostty.org/) - new terminal emulator by Mitchell Hashimoto. He posts occasional development logs on his site and is active on the project’s Discord server
- [Kindle](https://www.amazon.com/kindle) - nothing beats a physical book, but the Kindle's light and doubles as a reader for technical papers
- [Ultra minimalist wallet](https://www.amazon.fr/dp/B071L8ZKGK) - slim, no bulk, does exactly what it needs to
- [Espresso machine SIEMENS EQ500](https://www.boulanger.com/ref/1214874) - simple, reliable, been running strong for 2 years
- [AirPods Pro 3](https://www.apple.com/airpods-pro/) - switched over from Bose and honestly one of Apple's best products
